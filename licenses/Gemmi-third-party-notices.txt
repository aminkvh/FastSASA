Gemmi bundled third-party header notices
========================================

Gemmi includes small third-party header-only components under
`third-party/gemmi/include/gemmi/third_party`.

The full notices remain in the corresponding source headers. The key bundled
components in this FastSASA snapshot are:

- TAO PEGTL: MIT License, copyright Dr. Colin Hirsch and Daniel Frey.
  A standalone copy is shipped as `licenses/TAO-PEGTL-MIT.txt`.
- stb_sprintf: dual MIT or public-domain/unlicense terms, copyright Sean
  Barrett. See `stb_sprintf.h`.
- fast_float: dual Apache-2.0 or MIT terms, copyright the fast_float authors.
  See `fast_float.h`.
- sajson: MIT-style permissive terms, copyright Chad Austin. See `sajson.h`.
- tinydir: BSD-style permissive terms, copyright tinydir authors. See
  `tinydir.h`.
- pocketfft header: BSD-style permissive terms. See `pocketfft_hdronly.h`.
